# Privacy Policy for Your Pong

**Last updated:** 23rd September 2026

## 1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how Marcos Sousa ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in relation to the mobile game **Your Pong** (the "Game").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. 

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**Your Pong does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data.**

* **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to play the Game.
* **No Analytics:** We do not track user behavior, usage statistics, or telemetry data.
* **No Advertising:** The Game does not use third-party advertising networks or tracking SDKs.
* **Local Data Storage:** Game progression, high scores, and local settings are stored exclusively on your local device storage. This data is not transmitted to any external servers.

## 3. Children’s Privacy
Our Game does not address anyone under the age of 13, nor does it knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect any personal data, the Game is fully compliant with applicable child privacy protection laws (including COPPA).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
